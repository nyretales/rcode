Easing, side by side

Every ball travels the same distance in the same time. Only the easing changes, and that's what makes motion feel cheap or expensive. Tap a row to replay it, change the duration, copy the code.

Which one to use

  • Arriving on screen
    ease out
  • Leaving the screen
    ease in
  • Moving across
    ease in-out
  • Loops and spinners
    linear
  • A bit of attitude
    overshoot, sparingly
