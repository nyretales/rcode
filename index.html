↓ A little about the person behind R•CODE.

— Behind R•CODE

Made with curiosity.

I’m Hadier Al Ghanie, an independent web designer and developer from Indonesia. R•CODE is my personal space to turn ideas into websites, small web applications, useful interface components, and learning resources.

I work with AI as an assistant in the process. I decide the concept, visual language, structure, and final output. I prefer sharing useful things quietly: free code, practical prompts, experiments, and lessons learned along the way.

— A simple working principle

Explore. Direct. Refine. Decide.

AI can suggest, generate, and accelerate. I remain responsible for the brief, design choices, quality checks, and the final work.
